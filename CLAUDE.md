# discipline-kit

The kit ships engineering discipline — auto-loading rules, principal-engineer guides, review skills, methodology memories, and the differential gate — into other repos and `~/.claude`. This file governs the kit's *own* sessions, so the kit holds itself to the discipline it hands out (the rules it ships live under `claude-project/rules/` and do not auto-load here).

## Writing register

Prose in this repo — the README, the docs, PR text, and bash/python comments — follows the kit's own register rules (`claude-project/rules/writing-style.md` and `craft-documentation.md`) and `reference/voicing-document.md`. In particular: prefer the plain modern word to the erudite or theoretical synonym ("prompt," not "utterance"); no "X is Y" profundity epigrams ("the interface is the utterance") and no coined abstract nouns ("the utterance," "the ask"). Test: would a senior engineer say it aloud, or is it written to be admired? The full rule is "Plain words over fancy ones" in the voicing document.
